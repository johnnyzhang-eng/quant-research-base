## Instrument corrections before acceptance

Initial adapter runs exposed mistakes in our integration: the size-method signature and the one-shot decision calendar were mapped incorrectly; final-state reconstruction was initially called after the engine had cleared temporary arrays. These were instrument faults and were corrected before accepting the final shared-case evidence. Early attempts are retained locally and are not used as evidence against the engine.

The next bridge extends this explicit model with initial holdings, dividend entitlement before ex-day fills, receivable/payment, integral splits and delayed cash/share settlement. The first extension attempt exposed an additional instrument fault: an empty intent calendar's placeholder date liquidated initial holdings. The adapter now guards against that artificial decision. This failed attempt remains registered; it was not evidence against the native engine.

The aligned model adds integer sizing, registered fees/ticks, a known opening-capacity bound and terminal valuation. It still uses private Vibe engine hooks; installed source hashes and dependencies are captured. It is a synthetic fixed-target experiment, not the Vibe full strategy signal path or a broker test. Details and unsupported conditions are in `ACCOUNTING_MODEL.md`.

A wrong fee in a copied event stream is rejected by independent replay; five additional mutations change entitlement, net tax, settlement date, sellable split units or terminal noncash equity. Each is rejected. An intentionally wrong adapter subclass omits noncash assets through the actual engine path and is also rejected. A wrong cash-update sign in a disposable reference source copy causes semantic comparison failures. Original sources remain unchanged. No loaders or brokerage methods are invoked in these tests.

No formal historical strategy return, forward strategy decision or broker order is claimed by these counts. Local vendor snapshots, source permissions and unresolved data assumptions are separate from this public synthetic evidence.

- **6 paired-inference controls and4 bad-input rejections**: complete monthly pairing, net compounding, positive/negative/zero log growth, insufficient samples, retained insolvency and daily drawdown. Fraction arithmetic replays all fixed circular draws and percentiles; missing months on both sides, incomplete calendars, external flows, future perturbations and wrong-source benchmark addition are tested.
