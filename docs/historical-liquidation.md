# Separate hypothetical end liquidation

`execute_exit(data, reconciled_result, forward, native=None)` continues a terminal account into an explicitly supplied future window. It independently reconciles the study first, leaves its result and terminal marked NAV unchanged, and uses the existing `historical_class` over the same installed native Vibe source files. It restores actual terminal cash, integer holdings, share locks, pending sale proceeds, dividend rights, unpaid interest, wallet events, and the last calendar accrual date. It does not sell at the study's last close or reset the account.

The returned `classification` is `SEPARATE_CONDITIONAL_HYPOTHETICAL_EXIT`. `study_terminal` retains both original snapshots. `events`, `wallet_events`, and snapshots describe the continuation; `reconciliation_data` and `reconciliation_result` retain the full original prefixes plus the extension. `exit_wallet_snapshot` follows the separately recorded cash conversion. `realizability` is `COMPLETE_CASH_EXIT` only when shares, settlement, dividends, interest, and fixed liabilities are discharged and the required exit FX evidence exists; otherwise it is `PENDING`, with explicit reasons. This is an offline model branch, not a broker liquidation or a historical strategy return.

Forward version1 requires exactly:

- `schema_version: historical-exit/1`, `through` (an actual future stock session).
- `calendar`: ordered, unique `{trade_date, open_at, close_at}` rows. Existing history cannot change. Calendar coverage must include any modeled settlement due date.
- `bars`: explicit `{date, symbol, open, high, low, close, volume, open_at, close_at, available_at, open_quote_known_at, open_status, open_capacity_shares}` rows. Missing/unknown prices, quotes, statuses and capacity remain explicit. Raw history used by the volume screen must match the study source; the original artifact's rows are carried forward automatically.
- `actions`: future split or dividend rows, with explicit withholding and payment timestamp for dividends; old or duplicate actions are rejected.
- `terms` and `cash_rules`: frozen dated rules. Existing rules, funding declarations and clocks must remain identical; only future rules/schedules can be appended. There is no invented free funding route or unknown-cost zero default.
- `fx: {close_marks, execution_quotes}`: explicit nonfuture source marks and execution quotes indexed by session.
- `source_binding: {data_kind, evidence, payload_sha256}`. Evidence contains `reference`, `sha256`, `known_at`, and `basis` (`invented_control` or `reviewed_historical`). The payload hash is `canonical_hash` of every forward field except `source_binding`. The source classification must match the study.

Version2 uses `historical-exit/2` and adds `expense_review` and `fixed_expenses`, exactly equal to the original model2 review and schedule. `ExpenseWallet.restore_expenses_from_events()` reconstructs already booked invoices and remaining amounts. Remaining scheduled expenses continue to book and pay from their proper currency. An exit window beyond the original expense review's coverage remains pending; it never resets invoices or silently assumes zero costs.

Each actual future session attempts to sell held shares. Unsettled shares cannot be sold. C3 waits one additional actual session. Unknown/late opening quotes, unknown/halted status, unknown/zero capacity, incomplete prior volume history and nonpositive net proceeds produce cancellation reasons. Capacity uses exactly the preceding20 actual sessions whose volumes were available before the open, a 0.1% average-volume screen, and explicit opening capacity. Partial fills retain both unsold inventory and pending proceeds. Existing native close/reduction functions calculate stock cash effects, dated broker/tax components and settlement dates. Calendar interest and dividend payments continue across weekends before the next stock session.

After the window's final calendar cutoff, only available settled USD is eligible for conversion. Positive unpaid interest and receivables remain unspendable. Negative accruals and unpaid fixed expenses reserve cash. The supplied sell-side fixed USD fee is deducted before applying the bid; C2/C3 also apply the separate25bp exit stress. Conversion does not automatically sell securities or extinguish unpaid rights. Partial cash conversion can therefore occur while realizability remains pending.

`reconcile_exit(data, study, forward, result)` independently replays the full prefixes with Fraction, then checks the cash-only FX event and final wallet. It inherits the reviewed stock/wallet oracle without importing production amount calculations. It separately derives earliest eligible sale quantities, the preceding20-session capacity screen, cancellation reasons, one attempt per held instrument/session, fee/settlement cash effects, rights, interest, fixed liabilities, conversion and realizability. Public suffix journals must match the reconciled complete journals. Mutating a preserved prefix, fee, dividend payment, conversion, public journal, or completion label cannot receive acceptance merely because the native engine ran.

Finite controls run with `python -m unittest tests.test_historical_liquidation` in an existing Vibe runtime. Nine actual-native tests include a positive-cost known answer: split5 SPY shares into10, sell SPY10 and EFA5 with USD6 fee each, receive net dividend0.8, accrue calendar interest on settled cash, reach USD990.78, and convert after a USD1 fee at bid7×0.99 to final CNY13859.17. A partial-capacity answer preserves four unsold shares and USD400 pending proceeds, with final marked CNY13979.07. Model2 carries an unpaid USD1200 invoice, pays USD1000 after settlement and leaves USD200 owed; a future CNY100 expense gives final CNY5500 and pending status. C3's next-session delay and extra25bp FX cost are also checked.

Limits are explicit: the finite window must end on an actual stock session; missing forward rows/FX marks or missing horizon coverage returns pending. Data permissions, evidence authenticity, funding legality and real tradability remain external reviewed inputs. Restored native positions use the terminal mark as a computational entry basis; unleveraged sale proceeds and whole-account NAV are reconciled, while original-cost-basis native trade PnL is not reported. This branch does not extend the study performance sample, download a price, obtain a credential, or send an order.
