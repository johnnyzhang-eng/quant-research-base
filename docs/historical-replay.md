# Independent historical account replay

`research_base.historical_replay.independent_replay(data, result, monetary_tolerance="0.00000001")` independently rebuilds the linked USD stock and CNY/USD wallet journals with standard-library `Fraction`. It imports no production account, fee, interest, FX, or Vibe amount helpers. The report includes `passed`, discrepancies, the number of comparisons, and exact daily cash/equity fractions.

The input supplies `historical_model.terms`, `cash_rules`, scenario C0–C3, entry conversion, close FX marks, explicit optional exit quotes, and a performance-boundary FX mark. The source bars, sessions, instruments, initial balances, and corporate actions remain explicit. The result supplies contiguous stock and wallet event sequences, daily USD and CNY snapshots, the opening CNY snapshot, and native engine file SHA256 fingerprints. A constructed journal control may instead carry its explicit synthetic engine-source label. Fingerprints identify the producer's reported source files; this module validates their presence and form, while the caller must verify them against the files actually loaded.

Stock fills are independently priced from the opening quote and dated trading terms. Fee components, taxes, lot size, quote receipt/status/capacity, share and proceeds settlement, cash sufficiency, sellable quantity, splits, and net dividend entitlement are checked. C1 doubles declared broker cost and execution friction; C2/C3 apply the declared additional friction and 25 bp entry/exit FX stress. The oracle consumes explicitly bound intent weights; it does not infer a strategy signal or certify optimal orders. Each intent must produce one target plan. Complete opening NAV includes CNY assets converted with the latest nonfuture prior FX quote and is rounded to USD1e-8 with HALF_EVEN. Integer target deltas and requested order quantities are independently derived with floor rounding. Late or missing opening quotes use the last known mark for sizing, including an effective split adjustment, and remain unavailable for fills.

Wallet synchronization derives USD cash and receivables from its referenced stock prefix. Reported synchronization values cannot serve as new money. An engine interest posting references one independently calculated USD wallet credit; each nonzero credit must appear exactly once. Source cursors form a dependency graph and cycles are rejected. Calendar accrual checks both currencies every day, including weekends, using the schedule effective and known at the declared model clock, eligibility and tiers, ACT365/ACT360 basis, rounding, and posting specified as `daily` or `calendar_month_end`; an undeclared `monthend` alias is rejected. Unpaid positive interest belongs to equity, and unpaid negative interest reduces available cash. Neither receivables nor unpaid positive interest is spendable cash.

For non-session dividend payments, `payment_at` retains the actual economic time and `effective_pay_date` retains the declared pay date; the stock engine book date may be the next session. Synchronization checks economic timestamps rather than treating that later book label as a future payment. Snapshot cursors, settled cash and receivables must agree across the two ledgers. USD sleeve equity includes unpaid USD interest, and CNY equity includes both wallets plus independently valued USD securities. Prices unavailable by the calendar cutoff are carried from the preceding known mark. No quote dated after its use is permitted, including in a frozen-current counterfactual model.

The opening boundary uses its own supplied FX mark and precedes entry. Entry principal is a transfer of capital; spread and fixed execution fees affect subsequent equity. Terminal NAV retains securities and receivables. Optional exit figures value only cash currently available for conversion, with their explicit execution quote and costs; whole-account exit is absent while assets remain unliquidated. This replay does not sell securities, fetch quotes, place orders, authenticate funding routes, or certify real broker fees.

USD aggregate comparisons allow the declared tolerance, capped at USD 0.00000001, because the native engine retains floating arithmetic. The CNY aggregate bound is that USD tolerance multiplied by `max(1, FX mark)`. Fee components, interest, and supplied FX amounts use an absolute rational bound of 1e-20. These limits are explicit and do not become a cent-wide acceptance band.

The literal tests use invented inputs and manually stated journals. Their known answer spans a Friday–Sunday credit sequence, fee-version change, T+2 shares/T+1 proceeds, split before dividend entitlement, net withholding, and CNY FX valuation: final USD cash 607.11, USD equity 1007.11, CNY equity 7049.77. Mutations of fees, interest credits, source cursors, synchronization balances, entitlement, snapshots, and future quotes must be rejected. They establish finite accounting controls, not historical strategy returns or an official execution integration.

Run focused controls with `python -m unittest tests.test_historical_replay tests.test_cash_account`. Actual adapter acceptance additionally requires independently replaying the produced complete journals; passing the hand-built controls alone does not establish integration.
